Victory

How to play

Each round you and Rook each play one card from your hands, face down, then reveal them together.

The higher value wins the trick. The winner deals damage equal to the gap between the two values, plus 2 when the winner's suit beats the loser's suit.

Suit cycle: Flame beats Thorn, Thorn beats Tide, Tide beats Flame. Star is wild and never gives or takes a suit bonus.

Special cards change the duel: Foxfire is a mighty Star 10, Mend heals you for 6, Aegis blocks every point of damage that round, and the Trickster copies your foe's value and wins ties.

You draw one card each round. First to 0 life loses. If both decks run dry, the higher life total wins.
